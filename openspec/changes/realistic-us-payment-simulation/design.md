## Context

See proposal.md for motivation and scope. The simulator already has reusable profiles, deterministic daily schedules, historical Parquet publication, a current-time API runner and a Python protobuf-to-Parquet archiver. Reuse these boundaries rather than introducing more services.

Observed gaps are concrete: historical account creation timestamps are calculated relative to each purchase; live timestamps are anchored to the dataset start. Live customers are selected uniformly, browser values differ between paths, and scenario-specific device/network choices and forced foreign countries expose fraud shortcuts. The historical risk rules also differ from the gateway policy. The existing product is electronics ecommerce, not a representative sample of every consumer payment.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** build a reproducible, source-informed US electronics ecommerce population; share behavioral and observation semantics across history and live traffic; preserve bounded generation and current-time operation; distinguish assumptions from measured calibration.

**Non-Goals:** reproduce a real bank's joint fraud distribution, collect personal browsing/payment records, expand to other markets, introduce GeoIP infrastructure, train a model, or implement DP1 multi-source ingestion. Preserve the existing physical-volume requirement unless separately changed.

## Decisions

### 1. Versioned offline calibration, with explicit limits

Use compact calibration profiles in the simulator package, backed by immutable source snapshots or retrievable source references and hashes. Record publisher, URL, reference period, retrieval date, reuse terms, extraction method and applicable population for each parameter. Separate measured values, transferred approximations and scenario assumptions. Include calibration identity in logical generation identity and manifests.

The checked [Federal Reserve 2025 Diary findings](https://www.frbservices.org/news/research/2025-findings-from-the-diary-of-consumer-payment-choice) can inform US payment/channel context, but cover broad consumer payments observed in October 2024. Do not convert its all-payment frequency directly into electronics checkout frequency. The checked [UCI Online Retail dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail), CC BY 4.0, is a 2010-2011 UK gifts/wholesale retailer; it is a methodological reference for basket/repeat-purchase analysis, not the US calibration population. Select suitable US retail seasonality, regional population and browser/device aggregate releases during calibration; retain unsupported fraud, network-sharing and reporting parameters as explicit assumptions rather than inventing empirical backing.

Prefer public aggregates and synthetic sampling to copying customer-level records. No generation-time internet dependency; refreshed calibration means a new logical generation identity. A single imported fraud dataset would be simpler, but would not reproduce our checkout population or observation boundary.

### 2. Shared deterministic behavior with stable entity timelines

Extend existing profiles/schedules/behavior modules. Profiles hold fixed birth timestamps, household membership, merchant/category preferences, spending/activity propensities and home region/time zone. Deterministic dated changes describe device replacement, fingerprint resets, domestic travel, shared household connections and rotating mobile networks. Model sessions and campaign bursts explicitly instead of independent uniform purchases. Keep customer and merchant IDs stable across all days and continuation.

Use entity/date/stage-scoped random streams, indexed dated transitions and bounded campaign windows so requesting a day does not require replaying the full history. Worker count and task order must not affect outputs. Both historical and live runners consume this same behavioral schedule; a live run's bounded target throughput scales arrivals while preserving relative customer, time-of-day and campaign behavior. Live starts at machine time with gateway timestamps, without a calendar-gap limit or fabricated catch-up.

US-only means US customers/merchants, USD and domestic location variation in this release. Remove active foreign-country scenario branches. Preserve the existing electronics category scope; source-backed prices and seasonality must identify their population and limitations.

### 3. Separate hidden state from observable checkout signals

Keep latent household/device/network identities inside generation state. Produce observed browser fingerprints with sharing, resets and missingness matching checkout collection. Use the gateway's browser-family vocabulary and fingerprint/network normalization semantics in both paths. The existing simulated-client boundary supplies generated observations; it must not inject oracle-only knowledge. Calibrate observation loss independently from fraud intent, with documented scenario-dependent behavior only where justified.

Shipping location remains order data. IP country stays absent where the deployed collector has no GeoIP enrichment; it must not be inferred from shipping country. Domestic movement can still affect modeled network reuse and purchase timing without adding a fabricated location feature. Raw IP values and addresses remain generated and must not cause outbound traffic to those addresses. Add the optional timezone observations described below through root protobuf, HTTP collection/API mapping, generated bindings, Parquet/ODCS and consumers together. Keep other additions limited to collected observations and canonical attempt timing rather than exporting latent state.

### 4. Fraud intent, decisions and evidence are separate

Retain stolen-card, account-takeover, card-testing and friendly-dispute experiments, but generate overlapping legitimate/fraud amounts, familiar/new devices and network reuse. Keep an explicitly enabled obvious-fraud stress scenario outside realism claims. Campaigns coordinate attempts across cards/devices/merchants without reserved identifier ranges visible as fraud indicators.

Fraud intent is hidden truth, not the gateway decision. Historical risk outcomes must use the same versioned policy inputs and semantics as the gateway; Python does not import Go domain code, so parity is protected at the contract boundary with a small shared set of behavioral examples. Processor success/decline/unknown behavior remains a distinct mechanism. Live events remain gateway-produced.

Retain simulation-truth labels per attempt in the separate label product. Model chargeback reporting probability, delay and right censoring separately: no chargeback yet does not establish a negative, and a legitimate purchase can receive a friendly-dispute label without changing the underlying intent. Only succeeded attempts can receive chargebacks. Use explicit observed-label versus oracle-target selections in downstream handoff; oracle/scenario fields never become features. Live availability uses the UTC publication-attempt time, never a scheduled due date; count delivery only after broker acknowledgement and retain downstream observation time separately. The embedded timestamp is not a measurement of exact broker commit or consumer visibility time. Pending in-memory labels remain honestly reported; durable scheduling is not silently promised by this redesign.

### 5. New immutable generation with coordinated selection

Use a new algorithm/profile identity and an unused dataset version discovered at implementation time. Remove superseded runtime branches instead of preserving old-algorithm support. Keep old datasets and their accepted/failed evidence intact. Vault synthetic-card mapping must cover the newly generated method identities and behavior assignments before live selection; a shared count alone is insufficient.

Retain historical manifest-last Parquet publication and the existing archiver's protobuf decoding, raw transport provenance and immutable streaming completion manifests. This plan does not alter their recovery/verification policies. Keep distribution/cardinality comparisons diagnostic and integrity, schema, conservation, lifecycle-context, linkage and leakage enforced; source publication remains separate from downstream DP1 acceptance.

Measure a bounded generation calibration using the new algorithm before full generation: live machine CPU/available memory/disk, aggregate worker memory, scratch peak, rows/bytes per second, compressed output and upload throughput. Record estimated full-run duration/storage and resource budgets. The former calendar rewrite is not a generation benchmark. This is operational sizing, not a new local test suite; behavioral tests and image builds run in CI.

### 6. Explicit IANA observations and temporal feature semantics

The current checkout JavaScript includes `getTimezoneOffset()` in a device hash but exports neither an IANA zone nor a standalone offset. Collect optional `browser_timezone` using `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone` and `browser_utc_offset_minutes` using local-minus-UTC sign convention, the opposite sign of JavaScript's `getTimezoneOffset()`. Accept them through hosted checkout and the attempt API as browser-reported claims at collection time. Missing/invalid values become unavailable observations without blocking payment; retain valid contradictory zone/offset observations without converting them into trusted geolocation. A US shopper can legitimately configure UTC or another country's browser zone.

Validate IANA identifiers against a pinned tzdb release and record collector/calibration/tzdb revisions. Preserve the reported identifier and explicit offset; use a documented canonical identifier for derivation. Invalid or unavailable zones yield null local-calendar features rather than an assumed server, shipping or home zone. Offsets alone do not identify an IANA zone. Latent home/current zones drive generation but cannot fill missing observed fields or become production features. Browser timezone identifies a device setting, not verified physical location. Public references checked: [IANA timezone database](https://www.iana.org/time-zones) documents historical/DST rule updates; [MDN resolvedOptions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Intl/DateTimeFormat/resolvedOptions) documents the browser timezone observation.

Keep all business/availability timestamps as UTC instants. Record the effective rules version and derive local hour, weekday and local date at the event instant using the observed zone. UTC-to-local conversion has no ambiguous instant at DST fall-back; repeated local clock times remain different events. Distinguish rolling elapsed 24-hour windows from local-calendar-day windows, which can span 23 or 25 hours. Keep UTC storage partitions independent of customer local dates. Define these semantics for downstream consumers now; do not add Flink/Feast deployments or feature pipelines here.

Expose the persisted canonical attempt-start timestamp in gateway attempt responses and use it for attempt-truth occurrence and relative reporting-delay calculations. The current runner samples `now` before tokenization/payment creation, which must not masquerade as attempt occurrence. Keep attempt time, terminal payment outcome time, label observation/availability and downstream ingestion/completion time distinct. Historical chargeback occurrence must have the same business meaning as live chargeback occurrence; do not alternate between payment-success time and attempt-start time under one field definition. For attempt-linked labels use canonical attempt-start time, with reporting delay eligible only after success.

Define downstream feature cutoff as both event-time eligibility and availability to that consumer. A point-in-time event timestamp alone cannot prevent leakage from late-arriving or backfilled features; preserve the required availability/provenance evidence at the handoff. Use payment aggregate version for lifecycle order and Kafka topic/partition/offset for transport order; neither wall clocks nor UUID sorting establish global order. Retain the contracted microsecond precision in analytical columns and original protobuf bytes for exact transport replay. Inspect node time-sync/drift operationally before timing evidence; UTC formatting alone is not synchronization. Report clock anomalies without silently rewriting source timestamps.

### 7. Match the collection path, purchase lifecycle and population

The current live API runner supplies synthetic fingerprints and sends one attempt per payment; hosted checkout normalizes addresses, parses browser headers and fingerprints a trusted-proxy IP. Define one versioned observation profile shared by these paths: browser language/platform/screen/hardware combinations, timezone-related fingerprint changes, HMAC address normalization and IPv4 /24 or IPv6 /48 prefix hashing. Avoid independent random combinations that cannot occur together. Generate networks from a documented synthetic fixture space with realistic prefix sharing, IPv4/IPv6 mixtures, NAT/mobile rotation and missingness; do not present private fixture addresses as observed public geography or expose scenario-specific subnet ranges. Check real hosted collection against simulated projection in existing CI fixtures rather than running millions of browsers or adding a browser service.

Model repeated sessions, correlated category/condition/quantity/price baskets, domestic shipping moves/gifts, account onboarding and inactivity, and card/device replacement using bounded dated transitions. Keep money/item sums consistent with actual checkout semantics. Customer IDs represent merchant-supplied buyers, not universally resolved people; distinguish this observed identity from latent household/person membership. Do not infer issuer country, AVS/CVV results, billing geography, 3DS, GPS or other signals absent from current contracts. Missingness and collection revision can vary by channel/device/date, but never solely to reveal the target.

Share a purchase-intent/lifecycle plan between runners: same-payment retries after terminal declines, optional method change, delayed retry, abandonment/expiry and pending/unknown reconciliation. Transport retries retain command identity and payload; a new business attempt gets a new idempotency key while staying on the same payment. Do not mark a timeout as a declined payment or schedule a chargeback before confirmed success. Live waits/polls boundedly for canonical outcomes and reports unresolved work. Conditional processor outcomes must agree with the historical model at the same declared mechanism/configuration; preserve method identity when exercising a different synthetic processor outcome and keep control instructions out of payment features.

Maintain separate modes in the existing process/config: calibrated realism, scaled load and explicit fault/scenario stress. Record rate multipliers and label-delay compression; compressed time or arbitrary constant throughput cannot establish natural velocity/label-delay calibration. Distinguish business retries, duplicate exports, command retries and Kafka delivery duplicates in evidence. The existing embedded fault injector and archiver semantics remain unchanged.

The realism baseline uses the current collection schema and an explicitly declared duplicate-export setting; do not automatically carry over the old fixed 2% duplication or artificial user-agent cutover date. Enable intentional duplicate exports/schema-evolution fixtures as named data-problem experiments and retain their evidence separately from natural collection claims. Allocate the timezone-capable physical schema revision explicitly, updating contract/product references as needed; never relabel older files or force their shapes into the new runtime generator. Keep delivery duplicates preserved by the archiver/bronze regardless of how they arose.

### 8. Evidence for realism and honest downstream readiness

Deliver a calibration/observation matrix mapping each proposed feature to its real collection path, unit, null semantics, time basis, provenance and hidden-state exclusions. Report customer activity/tenure, repeat intervals, local-hour/weekday seasonality, baskets/amounts, sharing/rotation, attempt retries and label maturity jointly where references support it, not only independent marginals. Explicitly name denominators: customers versus active customers, payments versus attempts, fraudulent intent versus approved fraud versus reported chargebacks, and report number versus monetary loss rates separately. Never transfer a bank's aggregate loss rate directly to attempt-level fraud prevalence.

Include bounded sensitivity comparisons and diagnostic shortcut audits of missingness, fingerprint/subnet formats, card control ranges, collector versions and scenario composition. Strong separability by one generator artifact requires investigation; do not enforce an arbitrary model score or statistical p-value as an ingestion gate. Account for merchant/customer/household/campaign grouping and forward-time/label-mature evaluation in the training handoff. Training and online features must use the same predecision cutoff and prefilter survivor population; future processor results, decisions made after the prediction cutoff and oracle labels are not predecision features.

Match observable schema/collection behavior first. Population similarity remains an assumption until representative target-checkout observations are available; public aggregates alone cannot certify the joint fraud distribution. Review anonymized aggregate target observations when available, without building a new telemetry platform in this slice. Census retail/ecommerce, ACS geography and Federal Reserve payment-fraud releases are candidate sources; this research received HTTP 403 for those pages, so no values from them are claimed as inspected calibration evidence.

Calendar gaps between history and live remain allowed. Shared deterministic profiles can evolve through the gap, but unobserved purchases must not be fabricated as observed feature history. Record coverage gaps, warmup and right censoring for future consumers; do not equate missing history with zero activity. Keep behavioral diagnostics separate from DP1 contract acceptance and preserve existing evidence.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Public aggregates cannot identify the joint ecommerce fraud distribution -> publish assumptions and sensitivity ranges; describe the result as source-informed synthetic data, not production-validated fraud data.
- Shared networks and observed fingerprint collisions increase linkage ambiguity -> retain stable business IDs and hidden identity truth separately; never treat one observed fingerprint as a unique person.
- More realistic overlap may reduce apparent model/rule performance -> evaluate the combined prefilter/model population later; do not restore shortcuts to improve scores.
- Calendar and behavior correlations increase computation -> use bounded indexed timelines, explicit resource admission and measured sizing before full generation.
- Replacing the active model breaks old live manifest reconstruction -> coordinate the image, vault and manifest switch, retaining the prior image/config for rollback rather than an in-process compatibility layer.

## Migration Plan

1. Implement and review calibration/behavior changes with focused CI coverage; leave current source selection and deployments unchanged.
2. Measure resource needs, reserve an unused version and generate under the existing bounded/resumable publication flow. Preserve published v8/v11/v12.
3. Publish lineage, counts, date/label ranges, calibration assumptions and source checks. Request separate DP1 quality acceptance; do not relabel old evidence.
4. Coordinate vault mapping, simulator image and explicit manifest/hash selection. Exercise live behavior through the gateway and existing archiver, reporting unfinished labels and any observation differences.
5. Roll back by restoring the previous image/config selection without rewriting either source dataset. DP1 multi-source consumption remains owned by its follow-up slice.
